About this archive

This is a fan-made archive of the international dubs of the SpongeBob SquarePants episode "Help Wanted". It is a work in progress and is not affiliated with Nickelodeon, Paramount, or any broadcaster or dubbing studio.

About the data

Language and version lists are based on research from the Dubbing Database wiki (dubdb.fandom.com). Dub titles, studios, and cast are only filled in when they have been verified. A dash or a "not yet verified" note means the information still needs to be confirmed.

About the videos

Only versions with a video link can be played. The player at the top currently shows a test embed. Versions without a link show "Video unavailable".
